# Phone record view — manual verification

On a real phone (iOS Safari and Android Chrome), installed to the home screen if possible.

- [ ] Open a record from the grid: the cover tilts as you move the phone, no tap. iOS asks for motion access once.
- [ ] A NEW (sealed) record shows the shrink-wrap glare sliding opposite the tilt; a used one does not.
- [ ] Deny motion access: the cover stays flat, nothing breaks.
- [ ] Swipe the cover left/right: next/previous record; the new cover starts flat.
- [ ] Middle: album + year, artist, Pepe/Jenni bars and total, then tracks; the tracklist scrolls without moving the header.
- [ ] Scroll down: only the cover sticks; the details slide up over it. Once about a third of the cover is covered, Tracks, Info and Timeline appear with their tabs (tabs jump between them). Scroll back up: they fold away again.
- [ ] Changing record returns to the top. Liking a song with the details scrolled keeps your place.
- [ ] Tap the cover: the zoomed view leans and the small cover rests; closing it resumes the small one.
- [ ] A Timeline link (e.g. from the History page) opens the record with the sheet raised and the entry in view.
- [ ] Logged in: a small edit button sits top left next to the close button; it opens the form's first step; Delete is under the form's "‹ edit" root.
- [ ] Logged out: no edit button and no shelf (library) button; the dice looks like the collection page's.
- [ ] Rotate to landscape and back mid-tilt: no jump. (Most phones switch to the desktop drawer above 760px wide, so landscape checks apply only to phones 760px wide or less.)
- [ ] Reduced-motion setting on: cover flat.
- [ ] Liking a track (or rating) keeps the cover on THIS record: no cover jump.
- [ ] A short phone (small window or narrow landscape) stays usable: the tracklist has room and the raised sheet scrolls to Info and Timeline.
